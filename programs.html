Program 01 · Cars and light vans

Servicing and repair

Program 02 · Every single time

The mileage record

Every professional intervention has to have its mileage transmitted, and that stream of readings is what produces the document a seller must give a private buyer. A second-hand sale without it is void, which is a severe consequence by the standards of consumer law. The flip side matters to you as an owner: a thin history is a signal to the next buyer, so a car serviced outside the professional circuit arrives at resale worth less.

  • Every intervention
  • Legally required
  • Builds your history
  • Protects resale

Program 03 · Second establishment

Tractors and mowers

A separate registered activity and a different rhythm of work: machines that sit for months and then have to start on the morning somebody needs them. Servicing before the season beats recovery during it, by a wide margin and at a lower price. The same rules apply as on the car side, which means a quote before work, a phone call before exceeding it, and the old parts kept where you can look at them.

  • Per job
  • Seasonal servicing
  • Quoted first
  • Collection by arrangement

Program 04 · Said plainly

What we are not

We are not an approved vehicle inspection centre, so nothing we do replaces or anticipates the periodic roadworthiness inspection and nothing we say certifies a vehicle as roadworthy. Work on air-conditioning involving fluorinated refrigerant gases needs specific certification and gets referred unless we hold it. Waste oils, filters, batteries and tyres go through authorised channels. And the company was formed in 2023, which we would rather state than imply otherwise.

  • No inspection role
  • Referred where certified
  • Waste channelled
  • Formed 2023
